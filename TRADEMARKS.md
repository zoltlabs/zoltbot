## What you can do without asking

- Use the names to refer to the project accurately, for example "built with Zoltbot" or "a fork of Zoltbot".
- Build and run unmodified source for yourself or your team, and send changes back to this project.

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a modified version, such as a fork, a repackaged build or an app store listing:

- Give it a different name and icon, and don't use the logo or mascot.
- Replace the official identifiers: the bundle IDs (`com.zoltlabs.zoltbot`), the App Group, the Expo owner and project, and the hosted service addresses.
- Don't suggest it's official or endorsed by Zolt Labs.

To ask about other uses, open an issue or email zolt@zoltbot.ai.
